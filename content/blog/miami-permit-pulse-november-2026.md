**The short answer.** In October 2026, Miami Dade County issued 433 AC and mechanical permits, down 23% from 565 in October 2025. Permits for AC change outs, the replacement jobs that usually start with a phone call, fell 29%, from 196 to 140. Plumbing went the other way, up 15%.

If October felt slow, the county's numbers say you weren't alone. Here's what moved, what last year did next, and what to do with the quiet weeks.

## October 2026 against October 2025

| Trade | Oct 2025 | Oct 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC and mechanical | 565 | 433 | down 23% |
| AC change outs | 196 | 140 | down 29% |
| Plumbing | 448 | 517 | up 15% |
| Electrical | 1,393 | 1,200 | down 14% |
| Roofing | 1,074 | 945 | down 12% |

Source: Miami Dade County, Building Permits Issued By Miami Dade County, 2 Previous Years to Present, pulled Nov 10 2026.

## When the change outs come

A change out is a replacement. The old unit dies, the owner calls, a new one goes in. That makes change out permits the closest thing in the county's data to the calls an AC shop actually gets.

The pattern is plain. The peaks came in summer: about 200 a month in July and September 2025, and in June and July 2026. The low came this winter, under 100 a month in January and February 2026. The busiest months saw about twice the change outs of the quietest.

## What last year did next

Last November, AC and mechanical permits dropped from 565 to 378, about a third. Plumbing dropped from 448 to 346. Roofing from 1,074 to 797. The quiet stretch ran into February.

This November is starting slower than last year's did. In the first nine days, the county issued 79 AC and mechanical permits, against 93 in the same nine days of 2025. Nine days is not a month, so treat that as an early read, not a result. The December edition will have the full count.

## What it means for your phone

A permit shows up after the call, the visit and the paperwork. So permits are a late, rough read on how busy the trade is. They don't count calls, and a lot of repair work needs no permit at all.

Still, the shape of the year is clear, and it gives you a choice. The slow months are the cheap time to fix the leak. Who answers when everyone is on a job? How fast does a missed caller hear back? What happens to a quote nobody chases?

Sort that out in January, and you go into the July change out rush ready. Leave it, and you find out in July, with the phone ringing.

## Questions owners ask about these numbers

**How many AC permits did Miami Dade County issue in October 2026?**
433 AC and mechanical permits, down 23% from 565 in October 2025. That count is from the county building department only.

**When is AC replacement season in Miami?**
In the county's data for the last two years, AC change out permits peaked in summer at about 200 a month (July and September 2025, June and July 2026). The lowest months were January and February 2026, under 100 each.

**Do these numbers cover all of Miami Dade?**
No. They cover the county building department, mostly unincorporated Miami Dade. The City of Miami, Hialeah, Miami Beach and other cities with their own permit offices aren't included.

## How we count

- AC and mechanical: county permit type MECH. It also covers ventilation, ductwork, refrigeration and similar work, not only AC.
- AC change outs: MECH permits whose job description mentions a change out ("AC CHANGE OUT 3 TON," for example). It's a rough filter on how permit writers describe the job, so it will miss some change outs and catch a few other jobs.
- Plumbing: PLUM. Electrical: ELEC.
- Roofing: any permit whose job description mentions a roof. The county has no roofing type, so this overlaps other types.
- County building department permits only, mostly unincorporated Miami Dade. Not all of Miami Dade.
- Counts only. No shop or homeowner is named.
