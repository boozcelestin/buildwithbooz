There is a customer who already knows you, already trusts you, already paid you once and was happy. You do not have to earn their attention, outbid anyone for it, or convince them you are legit. They are the cheapest job you will ever win. And most local businesses never call them again.

## The forgotten list

Every business is sitting on a list like this. The people whose units you fixed two summers ago. The homeowner whose panel you upgraded. The customer who said call me when you have time for the bigger job. They are not lost. They are just not being reminded that you exist.

Meanwhile the same owner will spend real money chasing strangers who have never heard the name, when the warmest work in the whole business is a text message away.

> A past customer is not a closed job. It is the start of the next one, if you bother to reach out.

## Why it gets skipped

It gets skipped for the most human reason there is. When you are busy, follow up feels optional, and when you are slow, it feels awkward, like you are begging for work. So it never happens, and a list that should be a steady source of jobs just sits there going cold.

The fix is not a big campaign. It is a simple, regular reach out. A seasonal check in. A note when it is time for the maintenance you know is due. A quick message after a job to say thank you and stay top of mind.

## What this is worth

Winning a stranger takes ads, time, and trust you have not built yet. Winning back someone who already likes you takes a message and a reason. One of those is expensive and slow. The other is nearly free and fast, and almost nobody does it.
