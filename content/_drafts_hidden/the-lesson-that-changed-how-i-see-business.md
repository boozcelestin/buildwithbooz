I ran a marketing agency for years. Endgame. We were good at getting people more leads.

And I watched something happen over and over that took me too long to understand.

The leads worked. The businesses still struggled.

I would hand an owner more calls than they had ever had, and a few months later they were no better off. Not because the leads were bad. Because the leads were dying after they arrived. The call that went to voicemail. The estimate nobody chased. The job that slipped because one person was holding the whole thing together in their head.

More leads did not fix that. More ads did not fix that. It made it worse, because now there was more to drop.

That was the lesson that changed how I see every business. The money is rarely lost where everyone is looking. It leaks quietly, after the moment everyone celebrates.

I think about that every day now.
