Example of the Lead Response System

You call an HVAC shop. Nobody is free to pick up. Here is what happens instead.

Bayside Air
Missed call, 2:14 PM
Automatic
Hi, this is Bayside Air. Sorry we missed your call. What is going on with your system? We can usually get someone out fast.
Reply
My AC just died and it is brutal in here. Can someone come today or tomorrow?
Automatic
I can do tomorrow 8 to 10 or 12 to 2. Want me to hold one?
Reply
8 to 10 works.
Automatic
Done. You are booked for 8 to 10 tomorrow. We will text a reminder tonight.
✓Missed call turned into a booked job. In under two minutes.

Nobody had to be at the desk. That is the difference between a voicemail and a booked job.
